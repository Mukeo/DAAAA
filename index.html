📈 Algorithmic Complexity Benchmark: Greedy vs Brute-Force

Demonstration of why the Greedy algorithm ($O(n \log n)$) scales to thousands of tasks, while Brute-Force ($O(n \cdot 2^n)$) becomes computationally intractable beyond $n \approx 20$.

Benchmark Summary: For $n=6$, both finish under 1 ms. However, at $n=20$, $2^{20} = 1,048,576$ subsets must be validated, taking several seconds. At $n=30$, $2^{30} \approx 10^9$ subsets would require hours, proving why greedy polynomial time algorithms are critical for real-world operating system task schedulers.
